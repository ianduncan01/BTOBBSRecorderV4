BTO BBS, AWBS & Crick Field Guide

Quick reference for transect recording, distance bands, weather codes, and habitat notes.

1. Transect Structure (1km Square)

Each 1km square holds up to two 1km transects (typically ~2km total walk), split into ten 200m sections: Transect 1 = Sections 1–5 and Transect 2 = Sections 6–10. Avoid recording birds seen while walking between Transect 1 and Transect 2.

2. Distance Bands (BBS / Winter Transects)
  • Band 1 (0–25m): Perpendicular distance from transect line up to 25m.
  • Band 2 (25–100m): Perpendicular distance between 25m and 100m.
  • Band 3 (>100m): Perpendicular distance beyond 100m.
  • Flight (F): Birds in flight (not perched/landed in a section); log once in the section where first encountered.
3. BTO Weather Codes (1–3)

Cloud: 1 = <33% cover, 2 = 33–66%, 3 = >66%. Rain: 1 = dry/nil, 2 = drizzle/light showers, 3 = heavy/persistent rain. Wind: 1 = calm/light (leaves rustle), 2 = moderate (twigs/branches move), 3 = strong (whole trees sway). Visibility: 1 = good, 2 = moderate/misty, 3 = poor/foggy.

4. BTO / Crick Habitat Codes & AWBS Notes

Use Crick/BTO habitat codes such as WD (Woodland), SC (Scrub), GR (Grassland), AR (Arable), HT (Heath/Moor), FW (Freshwater/Wetland), CS (Coastal), UR (Urban/Suburban) per 200m section. In AWBS mode, use winter visit labels (W1–W4) to track multi-visit winter flocks and hedgerow/field changes.
